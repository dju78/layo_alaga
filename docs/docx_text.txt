Design DirectionThe product should feel elegant, premium, warm, culturally authentic, professional and easy to use.Use generous white space instead of filling every area with purple decoration.Use purple for important headings, primary actions, selected states and navigation highlights.Use gold sparingly for premium emphasis, ceremonial details and high-value moments.Keep most backgrounds white or very light lavender.2. White-Space Rules1.  Do not place content against the edge of the screen. Use at least 20px mobile padding.2.  Separate different topics with at least 48px.3.  Keep no more than three major sections visible at once on mobile.4.  Use a maximum of three detailed service cards per desktop row.5.  Keep paragraphs under six lines where possible.6.  Avoid unnecessary boxes. Not every piece of text needs a card.7.  Use dividers only when spacing cannot clearly separate content.8.  Allow important confirmation content to stand alone.◆Core design decisionWhite space is not empty space. It is the structure that makes the purple identity feel premium rather than heavy.29.
